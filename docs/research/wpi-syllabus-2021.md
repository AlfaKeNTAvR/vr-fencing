# WPI Fencing Club syllabus (C term 2021)

Follow-up to PER-17 (`beginner-epee-curriculum.md`). Researched 2026-10-18.

## Answer

- The newest public WPI program is the **C term 2021 F/E/S syllabus** (version 16 Mar 2021) by coach David C. Brown (USFCA Prevot d'Armes): 19 one-hour classes rotating foil, épée and sabre, assuming A/B term knowledge. It is linked from an HTML comment on https://fencing.wpi.edu/ via the "Overview: WPI C Term Fencing Practices 2021" Google Doc.
- Nothing newer is public. The A/B 2020 syllabi went to members through TechSync; current material is probably in the club Slack (invite only), myWPI (login), Facebook/Instagram (login).
- Every class has the same shape: 5 min intro, 10 min warmup, **15 min footwork**, 5 min theory, **15 min bladework** (target hitting, blade manipulation, attacks, parries), 10 min conditioning. Everything "deliberately with concentration, at slow to medium speed".
- The épée thread (classes 1, 3, 7, 8, 12, 16, 17) is the drill source for the app; see below.

## Épée content by class

Abbreviations: OG on guard, adv advance, retr retreat, rip riposte, circ circular, c-a counter-attack.

- **Class 1 (General, all weapons):** footwork: bouncing, adv, retr, jump, lunge, half step forward/back, forward reprise, checks, half and full lunge lengths, normal/accelerated/flying lunge, lunge from half lunge. Bladework: simple attacks (disengage, cutover, low-high, high-low), hand positions for hits; épée OG and parries circ 6, 4, 8/2; lateral, semicircular and circular parries.
- **Épée 1 (class 3):** half-step combinations, short and long lunge. Reaction: coach lunges, you parry circ 6 and riposte; coach step-lunges, you retreat and counter-attack to the top of the arm. Bladework: simple attacks to body, upper arm, thigh, foot, hand; engage in 6 or 8 against a straight arm and attack along the blade; disengage against an attempted engagement; retreat and extend to stop hit; parries 4, 6, 8, circ 6, circ 4 with transitions; sequence 8, 6, circ 6, 4, 6 with ripostes.
- **Épée 2 (class 7):** hitting distances (hand, upper arm, thigh, foot, body) with choice; riposte with extension, lunge or step-lunge. Feint to top of hand then disengage around parry 4 or circ 6; parries 2 vs 8; parries from a low-hand invitation; transitions circ 6 to 4, 4 to 6, circ 6 to 8; beat into 4. Choice reaction: blade presentation means beat attack, attempted engagement means disengage, target presentation means attack it.
- **Épée 3 (class 8):** front- and rear-leg (flying) lunges; lunge after adv, half adv, jump or half lunge. Theory: angulation. Direct feint then indirect attack (on half step, on step, with beat-feint); step-lunge compound attacks hand-thigh, hand-foot, foot-arm, hand-upper arm, outside hand-inside hand.
- **Épée 4 (class 12):** tactical half steps (draw an attack then parry-riposte; stop hit on arm and jump back; short attack, recover, stop hit; retreat, retreat, half step back, attack). Stop hits to inside, outside, top and bottom of the arm; remise of attack and of riposte as stop hits; step-lunge compound attacks.
- **Épée 5 (class 16):** adv-lunge, retr-lunge, jump-lunge; check then lunge; half step with fake attack then retreat and parry or stop hit and jump back; half lunge + lunge; half lunge + reprise + lunge. Beats (lateral 4, down, up), 4-beat disengage, change beat; parry combinations 4-6, 6-4, 6-8, 4-circ 4, 6-circ 6.
- **Épée 6 (class 17):** bouncing with half steps, double check, front-foot half advance and inverse half retreat before the lunge, false attack then real attack or stop hit. Theory: counter-time, invitations. Draw a counter-attack with a deep feint and parry it: feint under the arm to 6, inside the arm to 4, on top of the arm to 8.

## Other material in the same docs

- Foil and sabre classes (2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19) cover right of way, point in line, the chase, beats and sabre cuts; not used for épée drills.
- **Warmup and Conditioning** doc (23 Feb 2021): a 15-step dynamic warmup (jog, high knees, butt kicks, zombie walk, lunges, mountain climbers, arm circles, sword-arm rotation, balance) and a 4 x 30 s x 3 circuit (plank, side plank, bird dog, burpees, fast 3 adv/3 retr, split jumps, adv-lunge-recover-retr, and others).
- Each class lists YouTube videos to watch first (FIE finals, Szilagyi, Kharlan, Kauter, Massialas, Radical Fencing).
